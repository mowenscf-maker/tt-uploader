Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

5 Star Moments Uploader ("the Tool") is used only by the owners of the TikTok account @5starmomentsgta6.

What we access

  • Through TikTok Login Kit: the connected account's basic profile info (user.info.basic) and permission to upload videos to that account's inbox (video.upload).

How it's used and stored

  • The access token is stored only on the owners' own computer and is used only to upload the owners' own videos.
  • We do not collect data from any other TikTok users, and we do not sell, share or transfer any data.
  • No analytics, cookies or tracking are used by the Tool or this website.

Deleting data

Disconnecting the Tool in TikTok's settings (Manage app permissions) revokes access. The owners then delete the stored token.
